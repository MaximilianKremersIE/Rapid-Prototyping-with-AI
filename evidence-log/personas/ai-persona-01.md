## Shared Job to Be Done

When one month before university starts with limited time to search for housing, approaching course start dates prompt international students to seek to secure housing matching their criteria and arrive at university already settled and stable, rather than starting the year still anxious and searching.

## Behavioral Archetype

International student on a first housing search in Madrid, with a €600–900/month budget and basic-to-functional Spanish, who underestimates how long the search takes and so starts late. Near the deadline they grind Idealista alone, meet ghosting and scams, and sign whatever responds, giving up their criteria.

## Decision-Relevant Profile

- **Relevant context:** First move to Madrid for university; arrives late August or early September; told by an upperclassman to use Idealista.
- **Most relevant current behavior:** Starts in earnest 4–6 weeks before arrival, then multiplies search hours; few landlords reply; settles for a worse neighbourhood or quality.
- **Current approach or alternative:** Idealista only, recent-post sorting, messages and calls to landlords.
- **Decision-relevant constraints:** Budget; language; underestimating the search; scarce matching supply in the arrival window.
- **Product or research decision affected:** The product must surface enough real, verified, matching options early, and nudge the student to start early with a realistic timeline. Tested by [Card 1](../experiment-card.md#card-1-supply-audit) (is there more matching supply?) and [Card 2](../experiment-card.md#card-2-concierge-pilot) (do students sign ≥21 days before arrival?).
