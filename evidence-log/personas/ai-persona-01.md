## Shared Job to Be Done

Carried forward from [jtbd.md](../jtbd.md): when one month before university starts with limited time to search for housing, approaching course start dates prompt international students to seek to secure housing matching their criteria and arrive at university already settled and stable, rather than starting the year still anxious and searching.

## Behavioural archetype

An international student on a first housing search in Madrid who underestimates how long the search takes, starts with low effort and escalates sharply as arrival nears. They search one platform (Idealista), get few landlord replies, and sign the first available place in a worse neighbourhood or condition than they wanted, so they don't miss their arrival date.

## Decision-relevant profile

- **Relevant context:** First housing search in Madrid, arriving late August or early September. Platform choice comes from an upperclassman's advice.
- **Most relevant current behaviour:** Low search effort early, rising steeply in the final weeks. Messages or calls landlords on one platform, gets few replies, and signs the first place that responds and is available.
- **Current approach or alternative:** Idealista only, with basic filters and recent-post sorting. Peer advice. No other platforms.
- **Decision-relevant constraints:** Underestimates how long the search takes (an information problem, not a character trait). Group budget of €600–900/month. Limited or no Spanish. Few matching listings respond in their arrival window.
- **Product or research decision affected:** (1) Early nudges that show how long the search really takes, so the decision moves earlier. (2) The supply audit ([Card 1](../experiment-card.md#card-1-supply-audit)) decides whether consolidation gives this user more matching options, or whether scarcity remains regardless of search method.
