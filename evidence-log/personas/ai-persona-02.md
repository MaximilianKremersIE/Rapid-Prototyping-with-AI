## Shared Job to Be Done

When one month before university starts with limited time to search for housing, approaching course start dates prompt international students to seek to secure housing matching their criteria and arrive at university already settled and stable, rather than starting the year still anxious and searching.

## Behavioral Archetype

International student who starts early, calls landlords directly in fluent Spanish and follows a detailed guide from a mentor. Still meets scarcity in the first-choice area, but has time to compare and makes a conscious trade-off instead of panic-settling. Still uneasy signing from photos.

## Decision-Relevant Profile

- **Relevant context:** Native Spanish, budget €2,000+, a mentor who did the search recently, early July start.
- **Most relevant current behavior:** Searched 5–6 h/week from early July; signed 12 August, 2+ weeks before arrival; swapped Salamanca for Nuevos Ministerios.
- **Current approach or alternative:** Idealista plus direct calls, guided by the mentor.
- **Decision-relevant constraints:** Scarcity in the first-choice area; signing remotely from photos.
- **Product or research decision affected:** Whether verification and curation can be a paid tier, and whether start date, which the product can change, is what separates this outcome from persona 01's.
