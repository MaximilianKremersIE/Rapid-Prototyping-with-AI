## Shared Job to Be Done

Carried forward from [jtbd.md](../jtbd.md): when one month before university starts with limited time to search for housing, approaching course start dates prompt international students to seek to secure housing matching their criteria and arrive at university already settled and stable, rather than starting the year still anxious and searching.

## Behavioural archetype

An international student who starts the search early, follows detailed guidance from a recent mentor, and calls landlords directly in fluent Spanish. They still face scarcity in their first-choice neighbourhood, but they choose an acceptable alternative with time to spare instead of panic-settling. Remote signing from photos still makes them uneasy.

## Decision-relevant profile

- **Relevant context:** Arrives late August or early September. Native Spanish, a budget of €2,000+/month, and a mentor who recently did the same search.
- **Most relevant current behaviour:** Started in early July at 5–6 h/week, called landlords directly, compared options, and signed on 12 August, 2+ weeks before arrival.
- **Current approach or alternative:** Idealista plus direct phone calls, with the mentor's advice on timing, platform, neighbourhoods and how to talk to landlords.
- **Decision-relevant constraints:** Scarcity in the first-choice neighbourhood (Salamanca). Uncertainty about signing from photos. Depends on the mentor's guidance.
- **Product or research decision affected:** (1) Gives a comparison case for persona 01. If start date drives the outcome, early nudges become the core feature for persona 01. (2) Shows that verification still matters to a well-resourced student, which supports [verification.md](../../supporting/verification.md) as part of the core product. (3) A paid tier for this segment stays a HYPOTHESIS until willingness to pay is tested and the legal review confirms whether students can be charged at all ([legal-review-brief.md](../../supporting/research/legal-review-brief.md), question 4).
