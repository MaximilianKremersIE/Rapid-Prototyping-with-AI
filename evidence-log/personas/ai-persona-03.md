## Behavioural archetype

HYPOTHESIS: a private landlord or small agency in Madrid that lists rooms or flats on one or more portals, receives many enquiries, and replies to only some of them. Which enquiries they answer, and whether they accept tenants who can't view in person, decides whether student discovery turns into a signed lease.
