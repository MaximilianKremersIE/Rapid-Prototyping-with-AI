## Shared Job to Be Done

When one month before university starts with limited time to search for housing, approaching course start dates prompt international students to seek to secure housing matching their criteria and arrive at university already settled and stable, rather than starting the year still anxious and searching.

Carried forward as context: the student's job fails if this persona does not reply. Their own job is UNKNOWN.

Private landlord with a few flats or rooms, or a small agency, renting in Madrid. Gets more enquiries than they answer and picks the ones that look easiest and safest. Under Ley 12/2023 agency management fees are the landlord's cost (pending legal review).
