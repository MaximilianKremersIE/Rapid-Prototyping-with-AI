# Persona Hypothesis: The Paying Guarantor Parent

**Status:** Provisional Persona hypothesis requiring real-user validation

**Evidence base: none.** No interview in the repo is with a parent, and the student interviews as documented do not say who paid or who signed as guarantor. Every claim below is HYPOTHESIS.

## Shared Job to Be Done

When one month before university starts with limited time to search for housing, approaching course start dates prompt international students to seek to secure housing matching their criteria and arrive at university already settled and stable, rather than starting the year still anxious and searching.

Carried forward as context. The job is the student's; this persona is the person who funds it and may sign for it.

## Behavioral Archetype

Parent of an international student moving to Madrid who pays the rent and deposit and acts as guarantor. Does not search day to day but approves or vetoes the place, from abroad, without seeing the flat. Cares most that the listing and landlord are real, the contract is safe and the money goes to the right person.

## Decision-Relevant Profile

- **Relevant context:** Lives outside Spain; may not read Spanish; is asked to transfer a deposit and sign or guarantee a contract remotely.
- **Most relevant current behavior:** UNKNOWN. Proposed: reviews the place the student found and approves payment late in the search.
- **Current approach or alternative:** UNKNOWN. Proposed: relies on the student's judgement, photos and video calls.
- **Decision-relevant constraints:** UNKNOWN. Proposed: landlord requirements for a guarantor or larger deposit when the guarantor is abroad; paying internationally; reading a Spanish contract.
- **Product or research decision affected:** Who the product sells to and who it must convince. If the parent pays and approves, verification ([verification.md](../../../supporting/verification.md)) and contract checks must be shareable with the parent, and any paid tier may be bought by the parent, not the student.

## Validation interview questions

Ask about the last time they did this, not what they would do.

1. Think back to when your child found their place in Madrid. What was your part, from the first time it came up to the day they moved in?
2. Who paid the deposit and the first rent, and how was the money sent?
3. Did you sign anything or act as guarantor? What did the landlord or agency ask you for?
4. At what point did you see the place (photos, video, in person), and what did you check before agreeing?
5. Was there a moment you said no to a place, or asked your child to wait? What happened?
6. What worried you most about paying for a place you hadn't seen?
7. Did anyone else help you check the landlord or the contract? Who, and what did they do?
