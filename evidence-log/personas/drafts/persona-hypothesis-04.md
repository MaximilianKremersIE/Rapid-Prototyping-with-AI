# Persona Hypothesis: The Small Landlord or Agency

**Status:** Provisional Persona hypothesis requiring real-user validation

**Evidence base: no landlord or agency has been interviewed** ([opportunity.md](../../opportunity.md#current-evidence-base): "No vendor/landlord feedback yet"). The only signal is indirect, from students. Every claim below is HYPOTHESIS.

## Shared Job to Be Done

When one month before university starts with limited time to search for housing, approaching course start dates prompt international students to seek to secure housing matching their criteria and arrive at university already settled and stable, rather than starting the year still anxious and searching.

Carried forward as context. This persona is on the supply side: the student's job fails if this person does not reply. Their own job is UNKNOWN; proposed: fill a vacancy quickly with a tenant who pays and causes no trouble.

## Behavioral Archetype

Private landlord with one or a few flats or rooms, or a small agency, renting in Madrid. Lists on one or more portals, gets more enquiries than they answer, and picks the ones that look easiest and safest. Under Ley 12/2023 agency management fees are the landlord's cost, not the tenant's, so a platform that asks for money may have to charge this side ([legal-review-brief.md](../../../supporting/research/legal-review-brief.md), question 4; pending legal review).

## Decision-Relevant Profile

- **Relevant context:** Rents to students, possibly among other tenants; may rent seasonally around the academic year.
- **Most relevant current behavior:** UNKNOWN. Proposed: answers a minority of enquiries and ignores vague, foreign-language or remote ones.
- **Current approach or alternative:** UNKNOWN. Proposed: Idealista or other portals, an agency, or word of mouth.
- **Decision-relevant constraints:** UNKNOWN. Proposed: time to answer enquiries; risk of non-payment or damage from a tenant they can't meet; fees they now pay under Ley 12/2023.
- **Product or research decision affected:** Whether outreach can rely on direct contact or needs partnerships or landlord-submitted listings ([Card 3](../../experiment-card.md#card-3-landlord-response-test) decision rule); what an enquiry must contain to get a reply; whether landlords will complete verification Level 2 ([verification.md](../../../supporting/verification.md#verification-levels)); and who pays.

## Validation interview questions

Selected from [landlord-interview-guide.md](../../../supporting/research/landlord-interview-guide.md); use the full guide in Card 3. Ask about recent behaviour, not opinions.

1. How many properties do you rent out or manage, and what kind (rooms, studios, flats)?
2. Think about the last property you rented. How many enquiries did you get, and how many did you answer?
3. When you decide not to answer an enquiry, what is usually the reason?
4. Have you rented to international students? Tell me about the last time.
5. How do you handle tenants who can't view the property in person before signing?
6. What do you currently pay to list or rent out a property (portal fees, agency fees)?
7. If a platform asked you to confirm your identity and ownership to show a "verified" badge, what would you think? What would stop you?
