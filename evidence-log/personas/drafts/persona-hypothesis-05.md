# Persona Hypothesis: The One-Year Master's Student

**Status:** Provisional Persona hypothesis requiring real-user validation

**Evidence base: none specific.** The [interview index](../../recruitment.md#interview-index) does not record any student's programme (TODO (team) in every row), so we don't know whether any of the 15 were master's students. Every claim below is HYPOTHESIS.

## Shared Job to Be Done

When one month before university starts with limited time to search for housing, approaching course start dates prompt international students to seek to secure housing matching their criteria and arrive at university already settled and stable, rather than starting the year still anxious and searching.

## Behavioral Archetype

International student starting a one-year master's at IE in Madrid. Needs a place for one academic year only, so the lease length is a criterion in itself. Has a short window between admission and arrival, and no upperclassman from the same programme who has done the search recently, because the cohort turns over every year.

## Decision-Relevant Profile

- **Relevant context:** One-year stay; arrives for the programme start; no prior Madrid rental.
- **Most relevant current behavior:** UNKNOWN. Proposed: searches for leases of about one year and rules out listings that require longer commitments.
- **Current approach or alternative:** UNKNOWN. Proposed: same platforms as other students, plus student residences.
- **Decision-relevant constraints:** UNKNOWN. Proposed: lease length; short time between admission and arrival; less access to peer guidance than undergraduates.
- **Product or research decision affected:** Lease length becomes a must-have filter, and the supply audit briefs ([Card 1](../../experiment-card.md#card-1-supply-audit)) should include a one-year brief. If peer guidance is weaker, the product's guidance replaces the mentor.

## Validation interview questions

Ask about the last time they did this, not what they would do.

1. When were you admitted, and when did you start looking for a place? What happened in between?
2. How long a lease did you look for, and did that rule anything out? Tell me about one place you couldn't take because of it.
3. Who, if anyone, told you how to search in Madrid? What did they tell you?
4. Which websites, residences or people did you use, and which one led to the place you signed?
5. On what date did you sign, and on what date did you arrive?
6. What did you give up to get a place for the length you needed?
7. What will you do about housing when the programme ends?
