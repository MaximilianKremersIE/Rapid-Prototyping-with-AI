# Persona Hypothesis: The One-Semester Exchange Student

**Status:** Provisional Persona hypothesis requiring real-user validation

**Evidence base: none specific.** The [interview index](../../recruitment.md#interview-index) does not record whether any interviewee was an exchange student. Every claim below is HYPOTHESIS.

This persona matters now because exchange students arriving for the spring semester are the planned sample for the [concierge pilot](../../experiment-card.md#card-2-concierge-pilot) and the [student survey](../../../supporting/research/student-survey.md) ([recruitment.md](../../recruitment.md#how-we-will-recruit-next)).

## Shared Job to Be Done

When one month before university starts with limited time to search for housing, approaching course start dates prompt international students to seek to secure housing matching their criteria and arrive at university already settled and stable, rather than starting the year still anxious and searching.

## Behavioral Archetype

Exchange student coming to IE in Madrid for one semester from a partner university. Needs a place for a few months only, can't view in person before arriving, and may rely on the home or host university's exchange office for guidance rather than an upperclassman.

## Decision-Relevant Profile

- **Relevant context:** One-semester stay; may arrive in autumn or spring; returns to the home university afterwards.
- **Most relevant current behavior:** UNKNOWN. Proposed: searches for furnished, short-let rooms and pays more per month for flexibility.
- **Current approach or alternative:** UNKNOWN. Proposed: mid-term platforms, student residences, exchange-office lists.
- **Decision-relevant constraints:** UNKNOWN. Proposed: short stay rules out standard leases; furnished place needed; may arrive mid-year, outside the September peak.
- **Product or research decision affected:** Whether results from the spring concierge pilot can be read as evidence for the primary persona. If exchange students behave differently from the [Deadline Panic Settler](persona-hypothesis-01.md), the pilot tests a different user and its result must be reported that way. Also: short lets and furnished as filters; which sources Card 1 must cover.

## Validation interview questions

Ask about the last time they did this, not what they would do.

1. When did you learn you were coming to Madrid, and when did you start looking for a place?
2. How long a stay did you need, and how did that change where you looked?
3. Which websites, residences, offices or people did you use? Which one led to the place you signed?
4. Did anyone from your home or host university help? What did they give you?
5. Tell me about the last time you contacted a landlord or agency. What happened?
6. On what date did you sign, on what date did you arrive, and had you seen the place in person?
7. What did you give up to get a place for the length you needed?
