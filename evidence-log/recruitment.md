## Screening criteria

A participant must be:

1. **International:** moving to Madrid from another country for university
2. **First-time renter in Madrid:** has not rented in Madrid before
3. **IE:** studying, or about to study, at IE University

Record any exception in the index with the reason.

## Source check

Every quote or claim labelled REPORTED in the evidence log must trace to a real transcript or interview notes. Today the personas cite "chat" as their source, so **no REPORTED quote is traced yet**: each is reported in existing persona documents, with source verification pending.

To trace an interview:

1. Keep raw recordings and full transcripts in the team's private drive, not in this public repo (they contain personal data).
2. Commit anonymised notes as `evidence-log/interviews/interview-NN.md` (pseudonym, date, interviewer, format, key quotes with timestamps).
3. Change the row's Source check to `Traced: [notes](interviews/interview-NN.md), transcript held by NAME`.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending: no notes linked | No transcript or notes linked yet |
| Traced | Anonymised notes linked; transcript exists and the holder is named |
| Mismatch | The notes don't support a quote used in the evidence log; fix the evidence log |
