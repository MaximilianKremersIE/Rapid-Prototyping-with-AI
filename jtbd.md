# Job to Be Done

**Status:** Working hypothesis requiring real-user validation

## Job to Be Done statement

When one month before university starts with limited time to search for housing, approaching course start dates prompt international students to seek to secure housing matching their criteria and arrive at university already settled and stable, rather than starting the year still anxious and searching.

## Job to Be Done components

| Lens | Element | Working definition | Basis or status |
|---|---|---|---|
| How the job begins | Circumstance | One month before university starts; searching for housing in a specific location/neighborhood with clear criteria (good conditions, reasonable price) | Reported (friend's experience; pattern requires confirmation across 15 interviews) |
| How the job begins | Trigger | University start date approaching + inability to find anything matching criteria after weeks of searching | Reported |
| Progress | Desired progress | Secure housing that matches criteria AND sign contract early enough to arrive at university already settled and stable, avoiding arrival anxiety | Reported |
| Progress | Solution hired | Idealista — weekly 20-minute scrolls through feed with basic filters applied | Reported |
| Forces compelling change | Push | Approaching university deadline creates escalating anxiety; forces lowering of standards and accepting worse outcomes | Reported |
| Forces compelling change | Pull | Easy, quick overview of only relevant options without exhaustion of endless scrolling through irrelevant postings | Reported |
| Forces opposing change | Habit | Unaware of alternative platforms; entire social circle uses Idealista; it's free; market leader with highest trust and listings | Reported |
| Forces opposing change | Anxiety | Trust in unknown platform (will it actually deliver? will landlords respond?) and cost concern (worth paying when Idealista is free?) | Reported |

**Relevant progress dimensions:** Functional (secure housing matching criteria, sign contract early) + Emotional (arrive feeling settled, not anxious; avoid panic-driven compromise).

## Validation gaps

- Does this pattern hold across your 15 interviews, or does this represent one of several different jobs? How many students experienced deadline-driven panic vs. other struggles?
- When does the job actually start for different students? Is one month out universal, or does timing vary?
- What specifically needs to be "in place" for a student to feel settled and stable? Beyond housing, what else matters?
- Is the constraint primarily *time scarcity* or *discovery difficulty*? (This changes the solution direction.)
