## New international students at IE arrive in Madrid already settled

**[Name: TODO (team)] helps international students starting at IE University in Madrid secure a checked place that meets their criteria before they land, so they arrive already settled.**

*Madrid, September 2027 (hypothetical)*

**The problem.** Moving to Madrid for university means finding a home in an unfamiliar city, often in a foreign language, across portals, booking sites, agencies and group chats that each show only part of the market. Students can't tell which listings are real or available, landlords reply slowly, and time runs out. Many sign whatever responds in the final weeks, or arrive still searching.

**The solution.** Students enter their arrival date, budget, commute and must-haves once. [Name] searches several listing sources, shows each property once, and returns a short list that meets every must-have, each place marked with what was checked and when. It sets a sign-by date counted back from arrival and helps students message landlords in English or Spanish.

> "I signed for a room near campus in July, from home. I landed knowing where I'd sleep and spent my first week in class, not at viewings."
