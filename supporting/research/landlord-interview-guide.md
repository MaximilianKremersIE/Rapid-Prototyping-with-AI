# Landlord interview guide

Used in [Card 3: Landlord response test](../../evidence-log/experiment-card.md#card-3-landlord-response-test). Tests [assumptions.md](../../evidence-log/assumptions.md#area-3-vendor--market-viability): landlords respond to students who come through us, and see benefits in doing so.

Interview 5 landlords or agents, including some who did not reply to our enquiries. 20–30 minutes, in Spanish where possible. Ask about what they did recently, not what they would do. Introduce yourselves as IE students researching student housing, and ask for consent to take notes.

Notes go in one file per interview, referenced by pseudonym, with date and interviewer (see [recruitment.md](../../evidence-log/recruitment.md#source-check)).

## Questions

1. How many properties do you rent out or manage, and what kind (rooms, studios, flats)?
2. Where do you list them? Why those places?
3. Think about the last property you rented. How many enquiries did you get, and how many did you answer?
4. When you decide not to answer an enquiry, what is usually the reason?
5. Have you rented to international students? Tell me about the last time.
6. What do you need to know about a tenant before you agree to a viewing or a contract?
7. How do you handle tenants who can't view the property in person before signing?
8. What problems have you had with tenants who found you online?
9. What do you currently pay to list or rent out a property (portal fees, agency fees)?
10. What would you need to see in an enquiry to answer it quickly?
11. If a platform asked you to confirm your identity and ownership to show a "verified" badge, what would you think? What would stop you?
12. Is there anything we haven't asked that matters when you rent to students?
