# Student survey

Used in [Card 2: Concierge pilot](../../evidence-log/experiment-card.md#card-2-concierge-pilot). Questions ask about past and current behaviour, not opinions about our idea. Don't describe the product before Part B is done.

Before starting: explain the purpose, how answers are stored, and get consent. TODO (team): consent wording (depends on the [legal review](legal-review-brief.md)).

## Part A: Screening

Checks the criteria in [recruitment.md](../../evidence-log/recruitment.md#screening-criteria).

1. Which country are you moving to Madrid from?
2. Have you rented a place in Madrid before? (Yes / No)
3. Which IE programme are you joining, and when does it start?
4. On what date do you plan to arrive in Madrid?

## Part B: Intake

5. Where are you in your housing search today? (Not started / Searching / Have options / Signed)
6. When did you start searching, or when do you plan to start?
7. Which websites, apps, groups or people have you used so far?
8. Roughly how many hours a week have you spent searching in the last two weeks?
9. What monthly rent can you pay, including bills? (€)
10. Which of your criteria are must-haves, and which could you give up? (area, price, size, type, move-in date, other)
11. How would you describe your Spanish? (None / Basic / Functional / Fluent / Native)
12. Has anyone (a friend, older student, family) advised you on the search? What did they tell you?
13. Tell me about the last time you contacted a landlord or agency. What happened?

## Part C: After signing

Asked within a week of the student signing.

14. On what date did you sign, and does the place meet each of your must-haves from question 10? (Yes / No per criterion)
15. How many places that met your must-haves could you choose between when you decided?
16. How confident do you feel about the place you signed? (1 = not at all, 5 = completely) What makes you say that?
17. Did you sign before seeing the place in person? If yes, what let you do that?
